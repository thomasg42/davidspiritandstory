Before a story is printed

What if I do not know the birth time?

This book needs a birth date, a birth time, and a birth place for every person. It states those three as entered. It does not invent a time, and it does not turn them into a chart.

Are these scientific or medical assessments?

No. These pages are not scientific, medical, psychological, or predictive assessments. Numerology, Cards of Destiny, and Human Design stay off until a validated engine and the rights to use it are actually in place.

Can this app print my book now?

No. Print / save sample uses the browser so you can read a sample. It is not an order, a payment, or a press-ready hardcover. Bookvault, Blurb, Lulu, and Prodigi are a shortlist, and none of them is connected.
